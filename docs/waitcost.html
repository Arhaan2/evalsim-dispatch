When to stop waiting

A survey can persuade a dispatcher to pass a paying offer. With one slot left, can it learn when waiting for the next load costs more than booking now?

Which waits cost freight earnings?

For the same one-slot opening, compare three bookings. One agent skips, one buys and follows its previous booking rule, and one buys and applies the learned check before waiting.

With 50 paid cold-reversal surveys, the check stopped two costly waits on one tape played at two prices. Across those purchases the agent booked the same loads as skipping and paid 3.75 credits. On hot drops it stopped eight profitable waits on three tapes, giving up 80 credits compared with the previous booking rule at the same fees.

How to read this experiment

What was trained?
On its final slot the previous agent sometimes passed an offer that it would have booked without the reading. The new agent learns the return and downside of waiting for that disagreement. Seed 13 selected a penalty of 0.5 credits per credit of expected downside. The agent waits only if the adjusted gain is positive. A separate set of training shifts fits the purchase price after the booking rule is fixed.
What counts as a win?
On cold reversals, the lower paired 95% bound must exceed zero against both always skipping and the planner. On hot drops, retaining the win requires the lower bound against the planner to exceed zero. On original interrupted drops, all prespecified historical retention margins must hold. The six positive prices enter the primary comparisons equally. Free surveys are a separate control.
What counts as a new shift?
Eight unseen openings each have a hot and a cold ending. Each opening and seed shares its five-wave history across endings. The same underlying offers and potential readings repeat across prices. Bootstrap intervals resample whole seeds, keeping the prices and endings together.
Which comparison isolates buying?
Always skipping uses the same freight policy as the trained agent. The independent planner chooses its own bookings and survey decisions. The comparison without the waiting check keeps the purchase rule fixed and uses the previous freight policy after buying. The common-opening comparison gives each policy the same opening and unused slot.
Across the five training runs

All five fits beat the planner on cold reversals under the paired interval test. None beat always skipping there. No fit established a fresh hot-drop win over the planner. Benchmark your agent

Run your own JavaScript or Python dispatcher on the same shifts. Start on validation, then score the fresh test bank. Your dispatcher sees the price from wave one. After five offers, it returns buy or skip before it sees the sixth offer.

git clone --branch waitcost-v1.0.0 https://github.com/Arhaan2/evalsim-dispatch.git
cd evalsim-dispatch
node tools/reserve.mjs --suite waitcost --split validation --policy ./examples/lastslot-agent.mjs --out validation.json
node tools/reserve.mjs --suite waitcost --split test --policy ./examples/lastslot-agent.mjs --out test.json
node tools/reserve.mjs --verify test.json
