    Two overnight decisions

    On one Monday afternoon, both dispatchers moved their spare truck empty. RL sent it to Junction, where a spot load appeared the next morning. RL finished 43 credits ahead for the week. On a Wednesday afternoon in another week, RL left its spare truck at Junction. The planner sent it to Inland in time for Thursday’s contract and finished 44 credits ahead.

    How the study works

    Every morning and afternoon brings one contract due immediately and two spot loads. The Monday forecast can lag or misidentify the busiest depot. Both trucks keep their positions overnight. The simulator deducts loaded travel, empty travel and missed-contract penalties from revenue.

    The planner evaluates future freight possibilities using the public forecast. The RL dispatcher learns the future value of truck positions on training weeks. The greedy dispatcher takes the highest immediate net earnings. Every dispatcher sees the same current board, order history and forecast and has one second per decision.
