How the weeks are scored

Every dispatcher sees the same freight, closure warning and repair updates on a given week. Each has its own truck positions. Net credits deduct loaded travel, empty travel and missed-contract penalties. On-time service counts the share of ten due contracts served, including contracts interrupted by a closure. A repair lasts one to five half-days.

Training seed 13 was designated for release before scoring. The decision tests ask whether costly switches fell and whether the share of profitable moves caught held up, with zero allowance for a drop. Six further tests compare weekly earnings and on-time service with always short, the rolling planner and the constant penalty. The final test measures earnings over the planner on fresh double-return weeks.

Both policies start from two frozen dispatchers. One fitted on short depot repairs and the other also saw longer repairs. When they suggest different moves, the new policy can keep short or switch to long. The real closure length and future loads stay hidden when the fleet dispatches.
